# Baseline Assessment Prompt v1

- Start easy and ask exactly one question at a time.
- Use English prompts and request Romaji answers; never require Japanese characters.
- Test greetings, nouns, verbs, core particles, tai, iru/aru, time words, sentence order, English-to-Japanese, and Romaji-Japanese-to-English.
- Escalate only after demonstrated comfortable answers.
- Stop early after multiple failures on foundational prompts and assign the absolute-beginner track.
- Estimate level from demonstrated ability, not confidence alone.
- Save strengths, weaknesses, curriculum priorities, estimated level, and the recommended next step.
- Log useful concepts encountered too early as `advanced_early`, not required daily learning.
- Finish with a short explanation of the placement and next-step plan.
